Understanding Indian Sign Language (ISL) Recognition

Indian Sign Language is a rich visual-spatial language with its own grammar and vocabulary. SignSense AI bridges human signing and computing through spatial geometry.

📐

21 Spatial Joint Topology

Using MediaPipe Hands, we map 21 landmark vertices across your wrist, palm, and fingers into a normalized 63-dimensional coordinate feature vector invariant to hand size.

⚡

Held-Pose Classification

Unlike dynamic sentences, our current engine is calibrated for single held poses (alphabets, digits, key vocabulary), evaluating joint orientation via a trained Random Forest/SVM pipeline.

🛡️

100% In-Memory Privacy

No video or photos are ever saved to disk. Frames are analyzed in volatile RAM and immediately discarded. Only landmark coordinates are stored during training.
